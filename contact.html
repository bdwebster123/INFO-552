Other ways to get help

If you’re looking for one-on-one advising, the fastest path is:

  • Your school counselor
  • Your CollegeBound advisor at school
  • Your local Pratt Library branch

In a mental-health crisis?

Call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Available 24/7, in English and Spanish.
