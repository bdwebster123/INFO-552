Grade-by-Grade Checklist

What to focus on in each year of high school. You don't need to do everything — pick the things that match where you are right now.

A year-by-year list. If you’re behind on anything, don’t worry — most things on this list can still be done late. Talk to your school counselor for help catching up.

10th Grade

Sophomore year — learn the landscape

This is the year for exploring. You don’t need to apply to anything yet.

  • ✓ Tell your school counselor you’re thinking about college.
  • ✓ Take the PSAT in the fall if your school offers it.
  • ✓ Visit one college campus — even a quick tour of a Baltimore-area school counts.
  • ✓ Try a free summer program (CollegeBound, Maryland Ascent, or a local college’s summer course).
  • ✓ Start a list of careers or majors that sound interesting. It can change later.
  • ✓ Keep your grades up. Sophomore-year grades count for admissions.
11th Grade

Junior year — the most important year

Most college planning happens in 11th grade. If you do nothing else this year, do the things on this list.

  • ✓ Take the SAT or ACT in spring. Many test-prep resources are free.
  • ✓ Make a list of 6–10 colleges that interest you. Mix “reach,” “match,” and “safety” schools.
  • ✓ Apply to a pre-college program like Maryland Ascent.
  • ✓ Ask one teacher you trust if they’d be willing to write a recommendation letter senior year.
  • ✓ Visit at least two colleges in person or online.
  • ✓ Start drafting your personal statement (college essay) over the summer.
  • ✓ Look up scholarships you can apply for as a junior.
12th Grade

Senior year — apply and decide

This is the year you actually apply. The list is longer but spread out across the year.

Fall (Sept–Dec)

  • ✓ Finalize your college list with help from a counselor.
  • ✓ File the FAFSA in October (it opens October 1).
  • ✓ Submit Early Action applications by November 1 if any are on your list.
  • ✓ Ask for recommendation letters at least four weeks before they’re due.
  • ✓ Submit regular-decision applications by their deadlines (often January 1).

Spring (Jan–May)

  • ✓ Apply for scholarships — new ones become available every month.
  • ✓ Compare financial aid offers when they come in. You can ask schools to match offers.
  • ✓ Make your final decision and send a deposit by May 1.
  • ✓ Sign up for orientation and housing if you’re going away.
  • ✓ Apply for federal work-study or campus jobs if you’re eligible.

Behind schedule?

It’s never too late to start. Even seniors who haven’t done anything yet can still apply to many colleges. Reach out to CollegeBound or your school counselor right away.
