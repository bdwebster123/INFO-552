Deadlines

The dates that matter most for the 2027–2028 school year. Always check with the specific college or program for their exact deadlines.

How to use this page

Put these dates in your phone’s calendar today. Set a reminder for two weeks before each deadline. The hardest part of college planning is not knowing — it’s remembering.

Financial aid deadlines

  • Oct 1, 2026
    FAFSA opens The federal financial aid form becomes available. File as soon as you can — some aid is first-come, first-served.
  • Mar 1, 2027
    Maryland State Aid priority deadline FAFSA must be filed by this date to be considered for state grants.
  • Jun 30, 2028
    FAFSA final deadline (federal) The last day to submit FAFSA for the 2027–2028 school year, but don’t wait this long — you’ll miss most aid.

College application deadlines

These are typical dates. Always check the website of each college on your list because dates vary.

  • Nov 1, 2026
    Early Action (most schools) Apply early to hear back early. Early Action is non-binding.
  • Nov 15, 2026
    Early Decision (some schools) Early Decision is binding — if accepted, you must attend.
  • Jan 1–15, 2027
    Regular Decision (most schools) The most common application deadline. Decisions come in March or April.
  • May 1, 2027
    National College Decision Day The day by which you must commit to a college and send a deposit (if required).
  • Rolling
    BCCC and many community colleges Rolling admission means you can apply anytime up to the start of the semester.

Program deadlines

Program Typical deadline Notes
CollegeBound advising Rolling Sign up anytime through your school counselor.
Maryland Ascent Spring of 11th grade Check the UMD admissions page for exact dates each year.
BCCC Mayor’s Scholars Late summer for fall start You must enroll at BCCC and complete the FAFSA first.
Common Black College App Spring of senior year One application to many HBCUs for a small fee.

What if I miss a deadline?

It’s usually not the end of the road. Many colleges have rolling admission or late-application options. Community colleges almost always have spots open. Talk to your counselor right away if you miss something important.
