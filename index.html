Built for first-gen students

You don’t have to figure this out alone.

If you’re the first in your family to go to college, the application process can feel like everyone else knows something you don’t. They don’t. This site collects the steps, programs, and deadlines into plain language so you can move forward with confidence.

Baltimore already has people ready to help. We point you to them.

Programs near you

Three free programs that already help Baltimore students.

Apply early — some have limited spots, and the people who run them know exactly how to support first-generation students.

College advising

CollegeBound Foundation

One-on-one advising, FAFSA help, and scholarships for Baltimore City Public Schools students. Advisors are in most BCPSS high schools.

Learn more →
Pre-college program

Maryland Ascent Program

The University of Maryland’s program for first-generation juniors and seniors. Workshops, campus visits, and admissions support.

Learn more →
Tuition-free college

BCCC Mayor’s Scholars

Tuition-free associate degree at Baltimore City Community College for eligible Baltimore high school graduates.

Learn more →

Upcoming deadlines

Dates for the 2027–2028 school year. See the full deadline list for everything.

  • Oct 1, 2026
    FAFSA opens Federal financial aid application becomes available.
  • Nov 1, 2026
    Early Action deadline (most schools) Many Maryland colleges accept early-action applications by this date.
  • Mar 1, 2027
    Maryland State Aid priority deadline FAFSA must be filed by this date for state aid consideration.
  • Varies
    CollegeBound scholarship deadlines CollegeBound runs many scholarships year-round. Ask your advisor for the dates that apply to you.
