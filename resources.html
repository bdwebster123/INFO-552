Resource Directory

Organizations, counselor resources, and family-facing supports in and around Baltimore. Bookmark this page — you'll come back to it.

Local organizations

CollegeBound Foundation

College advising in Baltimore City Public Schools. Advisors on campus at most high schools.

collegeboundfoundation.org

Baltimore City Public Schools

District-wide resources, counselor directories, and a list of college and career programs.

baltimorecityschools.org

Baltimore City Community College

Home of the Mayor’s Scholars Program and open admissions to Baltimore graduates.

bccc.edu

Enoch Pratt Free Library

Free study space, internet access, and homework help at branches throughout Baltimore.

prattlibrary.org

University of Maryland Admissions

Home of the Maryland Ascent Program for first-generation students.

admissions.umd.edu

BERC — Baltimore Education Research Consortium

Research and data on Baltimore students. Useful for understanding the local picture.

baltimore-berc.org
